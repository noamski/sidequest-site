How one outing works

  1. Choose — A small mission near you (a place type, not a feed).
  2. Do — Go the same day. Leave when it’s done.
  3. Finish — Mark it finished when you’ve actually been.
  4. Save — One photo (optional) + one honest line. History stays free.

One small outing might look like

  • Bookstore — browse three shelves; leave with nothing or one thing
  • Park bench — sit long enough to write one line
  • Market stall — three things, then done
