How one outing works

One small mission. Then the next. Then stop.

  1. Choose — Pick a small real-world mission (a place type near you, not a feed).
  2. Do — Go. Same day. Leave when it’s done.
  3. Finish — Mark it finished when you’ve actually been.
  4. Save — Keep a memory: one photo (optional) + one honest line. History stays free.

SideQuest is an outing app: choose a mission, do it, Finish, then Save a memory to your outing journal. No streak required. Not available to download yet — join the waitlist for iOS.

Field journal

What your month looks like when you actually go.

Missions stack into a field journal — outing log, not a gratitude list. One photo, one line, repeated. After a few weeks the camera roll looks different because you did things, not because of filters.

Who it’s for / who it’s not

For

Adults whose days already feel fine — and could feel interesting. Quiet solo outings. Field-guide energy.

Not for

Healing. Mindset. Glow-up. Streak culture. A feed that wants your afternoon.
